Projects

Power Quality and Energy Monitoring Dashboard

Final year project

An ESP32 system that measures voltage and current, computes RMS values, real and apparent power, power factor and energy use, then sends readings to Google Sheets over HTTP. A live dashboard shows voltage and energy trends, forecasts consumption and emails alerts for sags, swells and poor power factor.

  • ESP32
  • Embedded C/C++
  • HTTP
  • Google Sheets
  • Data analytics

Bluetooth-Controlled Smart Lift

Embedded systems prototype

A lift prototype controlled from a custom Android app built in MIT App Inventor. An Arduino and HC-05 Bluetooth module handle lift movement and floor selection, using limit switches to know where the car is.

  • Arduino
  • HC-05 Bluetooth
  • MIT App Inventor

Microprocessor and Digital Systems Simulations

Proteus simulation work

Microprocessor systems built around the 8086 and Intel 8255 PPI, interfacing LCDs, encoders, decoders and other peripherals, and tested in simulation to mirror real hardware behaviour.

  • Proteus
  • 8086
  • Intel 8255
  • LCD
